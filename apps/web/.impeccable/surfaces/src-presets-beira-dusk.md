# Beira Dusk guest invitation

Scope: the guest page for the `beira-dusk` preset (`apps/web/src/presets/beira-dusk/`). Mode: Experience. Audience: wedding guests opening a personal link from WhatsApp, mostly on phones, teens to elders. Job: understand who invites them to whose wedding, when and where, find the venue, reply. Constraints: no pictures, no flying particles, no open button; calm music opt-in only; English copy; legible for elders.
